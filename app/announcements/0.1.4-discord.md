## Meadow app 0.1.4 is out 🌾

Every change in this release comes from your reports. Thank you to everyone who wrote in.

**To update:** open Meadow. When the banner says 0.1.4 is out, press **Update now**.

**What's new**

- **Troubleshoot**, at the bottom of the sidebar. It checks everything Meadow depends on, in order: this computer, your wallets and budgets, then each agent and its connection. It names the first thing that isn't working, with a button that fixes it or opens the place that does. Checking is free. A red dot on Troubleshoot means something needs you.

- **ChatGPT survives sleep.** If your laptop slept and ChatGPT's Meadow tools then said `Internal error`, the cause was the tunnel. It could die while Settings still said Running. Now the app checks the tunnel when the computer wakes, and restarts it if it no longer reaches the app. The address stays the same, so there's nothing to redo in ChatGPT. **Restart tunnel** is on the agent's card and in Settings if you ever need it yourself.

- **Fewer false alarms.** Test connection no longer makes a working ChatGPT sign-in look broken. The check's coloured marks no longer look like buttons.

===== Part 2 of 3 =====
- **Putting money in your wallet.** **Top off** now shows exactly what to choose at an exchange:
**Asset:** USDC
**Network:** Base
**Address:** your wallet's (copy it, or scan the QR code)
Send about $1 first. The app tells you when it arrives, then you send the rest. "What are USDC and Base?" explains both in plain words.

- **Where to buy USDC on Base depends on your country.** This page says what has worked where, and we'll keep adding to it: <https://meadowprotocol.com/v2/get-usdc>

- **USDC on the wrong network isn't lost.** If you sent USDC on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, or BNB Smart Chain by mistake, the app now finds it and shows it on the wallet's card. It's still yours, but the app can only use USDC on Base.

- **A notification when USDC arrives** in one of your wallets.

- **Clearer refusals.** Inviting an agent who's already in the room now says so, instead of `invalid_membership`.

- **The ChatGPT setup steps** match ChatGPT's real menus: Plugins, then Add, then Create MCP app.
