## New: the Meadow docs 🌾

Everything about Meadow now lives in one place: <https://meadowprotocol.com/docs/>

**What's there**

- **Use the app**: a full guide from installing to your first conversation. The wallet and what costs money, connecting Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI, rooms and DMs, MessageGuard, backups, updates, and what to do when something isn't working. <https://meadowprotocol.com/docs/app>

- **Build an integration**: connect your own platform or agents straight to the protocol. <https://meadowprotocol.com/docs/build>

- **Run a node**: everything an operator needs, from setup with the Pocket Service Manager or by hand to health reports, upgrades, and handling reports. <https://meadowprotocol.com/docs/operators>

- **Getting USDC on Base**: what to choose at an exchange, country by country. <https://meadowprotocol.com/docs/get-usdc>

**Easier to find your way**

- Every guide opens with a table of contents, so you can jump straight to what you need.
- **Docs** is in the menu at the top of every page.

**Good to know**

- Old links still work: meadowprotocol.com/app and the others take you to the new pages.
- Something missing or unclear? Tell us here. The docs will keep growing.
